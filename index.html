What it does

Watch it smooth, or keep a smooth copy

Live in mpv

Drop a video on the window and it plays in your own mpv with new frames: double, 60 fps, or your display's rate.

Picked for your computer

A speed test measures each engine once per GPU. Automatic takes the best one that keeps up, and never stutters on purpose.

4K and high refresh

mvgpu, ButterEye's own Vulkan motion engine, smooths 4K to 120 fps with room to spare on a modern GPU.

Smooth copies

Save a smoothed file in the background, with the audio, subtitles and chapters kept. HDR10 stays HDR10.

Your mpv, untouched

ButterEye never edits your mpv.conf. A small line in mpv says what it is doing; Copy log says why if it can't.

Explains itself

System checks name every missing piece with the command that fixes it. A buttereye command does everything the window does.

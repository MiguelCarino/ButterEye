Smooth motion for mpv

ButterEye

Smoother motion for the videos you play in mpv.

New in-between frames, live while you watch or saved as a copy. Free software for Fedora Linux.

  • Live in your own mpv — double, 60 fps or your display's rate; your mpv.conf is never edited.
  • Picked for your computer — RIFE on the GPU, mvgpu for 4K and high refresh, MVTools on the CPU; a speed test chooses.
  • Smooth copies — in the background, with audio, subtitles and chapters; HDR10 stays HDR10.
  • Explains itself — system checks with the fix, a small line in mpv, Copy log when something goes wrong.
